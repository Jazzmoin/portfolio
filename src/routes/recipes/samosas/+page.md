## Samosas

### Ingredients

- Spring roll wrappers (cut into thirds to make long strips)
- filling
    - 3kg potatoes (diced)
    - 3 big onions (diced)
    - 8 cloves of garlic
    - Handful of curry leaves (chopped)
    - 1 tbsp cumin
    - 2 tbsp curry powder
    - 1/2 tsp msg

### Method

1. Boil potatoes until soft
2. Oil pan and saute all other ingredients
3. Add potatoes to rest of ingredient and mix until incorporated
4. Stuff into spring roll wrappers
5. Fry

## Chicken Wontons

### Ingredients

- Wonton wrappers
- **Filling:**
    - 1 kg chicken (minced or finely chopped)
    - 5 tbsp soy sauce
    - 2 cloves garlic, minced
    - 2 tbsp tapioca starch
    - Pepper
    - Spring onion, finely sliced
- **Glue:**
    - 2 tbsp flour
    - 2 tbsp water

### Method

1. Combine all filling ingredients in a bowl and mix well.
2. In a separate bowl, mix flour and water to form a thick paste (this will act as glue).
3. Place a teaspoon of filling in the centre of each wonton wrapper.
4. Fold the wrapper diagonally to form a triangle, pressing edges to seal.
5. Bring the two top corners together, apply a small amount of glue, and press to seal into the classic wonton shape.

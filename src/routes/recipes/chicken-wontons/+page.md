<div style="padding-top:20px">

  <h2 style="padding-bottom: 25px">Chicken Wontons</h2>

  <h3 style="border-bottom: 2px solid #ddd; padding-bottom: 5px;">Ingredients</h3>

  <ul style="padding-left: 20px;">
    <li>Wonton wrappers</li>
    <li><strong>Filling:</strong>
      <ul style="padding-left: 20px; margin-top: 5px;">
        <li>1kg chicken (minced or finely chopped)</li>
        <li>5 tbsp soy sauce</li>
        <li>2 cloves garlic, minced</li>
        <li>2 tbsp tapioca starch</li>
        <li>Pepper</li>
        <li>Spring onion, finely sliced</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li><strong>Glue:</strong>
      <ul style="padding-left: 20px; margin-top: 5px;">
        <li>2 tbsp flour</li>
        <li>2 tbsp water</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
  </ul>

  <h3 style="border-bottom: 2px solid #ddd; padding-bottom: 5px; margin-top: 20px;">Method</h3>

  <ol style="padding-left: 25px;">
    <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">
      Combine all filling ingredients in a bowl and mix well.
    </li>
    <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">
      In a separate bowl, mix flour and water to form a thick paste (this will act as glue).
    </li>
    <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">
      Place a teaspoon of filling in the centre of each wonton wrapper.
    </li>
    <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">
      Fold the wrapper diagonally to form a triangle, pressing edges to seal.
    </li>
    <li>
      Bring the two top corners together, apply a small amount of glue, and press to seal into the classic wonton shape.
    </li>
  </ol>

</div>

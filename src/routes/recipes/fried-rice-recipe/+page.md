


## Ingredients

Chicken cut into little pieces (15mm x 15mm x 15-45mm) coated in light soy and oyster sauce

Prawns covered in white pepper and light soy

Chinese pork sausage(???) x1

Rice (jasmine) cooked in rice cooker (with Chinese pork saussage). Ideally day old rice.

Carrot cut into 8mm cubes

Corn

Spring onion (lots)

Peas

Eggs


## Instructions 

Cook chicken and brawns in batches in a wok at max heat unit just done. Reserve in bowl

![[IMG_20260503_174650_686.jpg]]

Clean wok, Max heat

Heat oil until smoking

Add carrot, corn, peas

Salt

Cook for 3 minutes

Add cut up Chinese sausages

![[IMG_20260503_175500_480.jpg]]

Cook for 3 more minutes

Remove veg to meat bowl

Heat more oil

Three eggs in wok, salt and then scramble

While eggs are still a bit uncooked, put a bunch of rice (approx 3.5 cups of cooked rice, as much as the wok can fit) and some soy cause in the wok

Add a giant handful of spring onion

Cook for 2 minutes stiring constantly

Add some of the meat veg mix

Cook for 2 more minutes, stirring aggressivley to mix

Repeat the above process until finished
